## Typography

DOE communications use a focused set of typefaces applied with consistent composition, scale, and hierarchy.

### Sans Serif Typefaces

| Typeface | Role | Approved Weights |
|----------|------|-----------------|
| **Figtree** | Preferred (web/digital only — not available in Microsoft Office) | Light, Light Italic, Regular, Italic, Medium, Medium Italic, Semibold, Semibold Italic, Bold, Bold Italic, Extrabold, Extrabold Italic, Black, Black Italic |
| **Arial** | Alternate (use for Office documents, PPTX, DOCX) | Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold Italic |

### Serif Typefaces

| Typeface | Role | Approved Weights |
|----------|------|-----------------|
| **STIX Two Text** | Preferred (web/digital only — not available in Microsoft Office) | Regular, Italic, Medium, Medium Italic, Semibold, Semibold Italic, Bold, Bold Italic |
| **Times New Roman** | Alternate (use for Office documents) | Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold Italic |

### Practical Guidance for Document Creation

When creating DOCX or PPTX files, use **Arial** (sans serif) and **Times New Roman** (serif). Figtree and STIX Two Text are for web/digital contexts only and are not bundled with Microsoft Office.

- Primary palette should dominate all materials
- Midnight Blue is the primary background color for covers and section headers
- Cardinal Red is reserved for emphasis and alerts — use sparingly
- Light Blue and Ivory work as subtle background fills
- Dark Gray is the standard body text color on light backgrounds
- Maintain sufficient contrast for accessibility (WCAG AA minimum)

## Official Logo

Released February 2025. This is the preferred logo for all DOE communications.

### Elements

The logo consists of:
1. **The official department seal** (circular emblem)
2. **The logotype** ("U.S. DEPARTMENT *of* ENERGY")

These elements are locked together — do not separate, alter, or recreate them.

### Arrangements

| Arrangement | When to Use | File |
|-------------|-------------|------|
| **Horizontal Condensed** (PREFERRED) | Most cases — documents, slides, web | `doe-logo-color-horizontal.png` |
| **Horizontal Extended** | Running headers/footers in documents | (same horizontal variant, scaled) |
| **Vertical Condensed** | Where vertical presentation is needed | `doe-logo-color-vertical.png` |

### Colorways

Each arrangement is available in:
- **Full-color** — color seal with blue lettering (standard use)
- **Full-color reverse** — color seal with white lettering (on dark backgrounds)
- **One-color blue** — all-blue monochrome
- **One-color reverse** — all-white monochrome (on dark backgrounds)

Available logo files in this skill:
- `doe-logo-color-horizontal.png` — Full-color, horizontal (preferred)
- `doe-logo-white-horizontal.png` — All-white, horizontal (for dark backgrounds)
- `doe-logo-color-vertical.png` — Full-color, vertical

### Clearspace

Maintain clearspace of **0.5x** around the logo, where x = the height of the seal in the logo.

## Logo Usage Rules

### DO NOT:

- Alter the proportions of the logo
- Alter the colorways of the logo
- Add elements to the logo
- Enclose the logo in a box or shape of any color
- Alter the proportions or placement of the seal or logotype
- Use the retired "bolt" logo (green lightning bolt ENERGY wordmark)
- Remove the seal from the logotype
- Use the seal alone (rare exceptions require BrandManager approval)
- Place the logo where legibility or definition is compromised

### Retired Logo

The "bolt" Energy logo (green wordmark with lightning bolt) is **retired**. Do not use it.

## Logo Lock-ups for Program Offices

Program offices pair their name with the DOE logo in a "lock-up." Lock-ups with the seal are preferred.

### Lock-up Arrangements (with seal)

**Horizontal** (most common):
```
[DOE Seal] U.S. DEPARTMENT  | Program Office
           of ENERGY        | Name Here
```

**Stacked**:
```
[DOE Seal] U.S. DEPARTMENT
           of ENERGY
           Program Office Name Here
```

**Vertical**:
```
        [DOE Seal]
  U.S. DEPARTMENT of ENERGY
  Program Office Name Here
```

### Lock-ups without the seal

For rare cases where the seal is not ideal, text-only lock-ups are acceptable:

**Horizontal**: `U.S. DEPARTMENT of ENERGY | Program Office Name Here`
**Vertical**:
```
U.S. DEPARTMENT of ENERGY
Program Office Name Here
```

### Important

- Do not attempt to recreate logo lock-ups manually
- To obtain an official lock-up for a program office, contact BrandManager@hq.doe.gov
- Lock-ups are available in full-color, one-color, and reverse colorways

## PowerPoint Presentation Templates

The official template (`doe-powerpoint-template.pptx`) includes these slide layouts:

| Layout | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Cover** | Title slide with DOE seal on dark blue background |
| **Cover Alternate** | White background title slide with DOE logo |
| **Content** | Standard content slide with header bar |
| **Content Two Columns** | Two-column layout for side-by-side content |
| **Content Alternate** | Clean content layout |
| **Section Break Alternate** | Section divider with title/subtitle |
| **End** | Closing slide with DOE logo on dark background |

### Usage

- Use the official template for all DOE presentations
- Apply DOE brand colors only (see Color Palettes above)
- Use Arial for all text in presentations (sans serif fallback)
- Use Times New Roman for formal/serif text needs
- Maintain consistent use of layouts — do not create ad-hoc slide designs that break the template patterns

## Fact Sheet Templates

Official fact sheet templates are available in one-page, two-page, and four-page formats.

### One-Page Layout (reference: `doe-fact-sheet-1page.pdf`)

- DOE logo in top-left corner
- Title/headline area with accent color
- Body content with professional typography
- Image placement area
- Footer with DOE branding

### Key Design Principles for Fact Sheets

- Follow the established template layouts precisely
- Use DOE brand colors for headers, accents, and callouts
- Maintain generous margins and spacing
- Use professional photography where applicable
- Include the DOE logo in the prescribed position
